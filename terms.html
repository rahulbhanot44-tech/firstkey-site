What FirstKey is

Advice in FirstKey is general information

Reminders, tips, checklists and answers from the AI helper are general information to help you decide what to do. They can be wrong or miss something about your home. They don't replace a licensed professional, a home inspection, or the maker's instructions.

  • You decide what to do, and whether to do it yourself.
  • Don't do work you're not qualified for. In Ontario, gas work needs a TSSA-registered contractor, and most electrical work needs an ESA-licensed electrical contractor.
  • Reminders depend on your phone's settings and connection, so they may not always arrive. Keep your own eye on anything important.

In an emergency, call 911. If you smell gas, see fire or hear a CO alarm, get everyone out first, then call 911 and your gas company from outside. Don't rely on FirstKey in an emergency.
